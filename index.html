What you type stays yours

Keyboard Goblin never reads your letters, words or messages. It only uses the position of the pressed key to place an effect. What you type is never saved, uploaded or analyzed.

Questions

Does Keyboard Goblin record what I type?

No. It only uses the position of the key you pressed to place an effect. Your letters, words and messages are never saved, uploaded or analyzed.

Does it stay above other apps?

Yes. It is designed to stay above other apps while letting clicks pass through, so you can keep working normally.

Can I make my own theme?

Yes. The editor that made the built-in themes is included. Start from a template or a blank theme, no coding required.

Can I use my own pictures and sounds?

Yes, as long as you made them or have permission to use them.

Can I share my theme?

Yes, through Steam Workshop when the Workshop release is available.

Will it slow down my computer?

It is designed to be lightweight, and each theme can limit how many things are on screen at once.

Does it work with multiple monitors?

Yes. The effects appear on the screen you are currently using.
